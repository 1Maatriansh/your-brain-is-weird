# PRD.md — Project Requirements Document
## Project: YOUR BRAIN IS WEIRD (Interactive Cognitive Neuroscience Lab)

---

## 1. Executive Summary
**YOUR BRAIN IS WEIRD** is a premier, highly interactive, digital laboratory and publication dedicated to exploring the counterintuitive, surprising, and often bizarre realities of the human brain. The website departs radically from online textbooks and generic AI templates, transforming visitors from passive readers into active participants who directly experience their own sensory and cognitive errors.

---

## 2. Target Audience
1. **Curious General Audience**: Individuals captivated by psychology, illusions, behavioral economics, and brain oddities.
2. **Students & Educators**: High school and university students/educators seeking authentic, interactive demonstrations of cognitive principles.
3. **Designers & Technologists**: UX practitioners, developers, and product builders wanting to understand human attention, perception, and digital habit loops.
4. **Knowledge Workers**: Professionals navigating attention fragmentation, decision fatigue, and cognitive biases in modern life.

---

## 3. Core Value Proposition
- **Show, Don't Just Tell**: Instead of asserting "the brain fills in blind spots", the platform forces the visitor's optic nerve blind spot to make an object disappear in real time.
- **Scientific Integrity**: Clear demarcation of scientific consensus using evidence badges (`[ESTABLISHED]`, `[PLAUSIBLE]`, `[DEBATED]`, `[MYTH BUSTER]`). No pop-psychology folklore.
- **Deep Interconnectedness**: 120 curated concepts across 6 regions linked in a persistent rabbit-hole graph.
- **Apple-Grade Aesthetic**: Luxurious dark styling (`#000000` / `#0a0a0a`), responsive Cupertino controls, fluid micro-interactions, and sound synthesis feedback.

---

## 4. Key Functional Features

### 4.1 Intro Experience (`intro_ui`)
- Full-viewport scroll-scrubbed background video rendering brain-like folds and ambient light particles.
- Frosted glass chips (`bg-white/15 backdrop-blur-md`), Inter typography, left-accent mono badges.
- Section One (Hero, services `/ PERCEPTION`, `/ COGNITION`, `/ ATTENTION HACKS`, researcher portrait card).
- 80vh scroll spacer to provide depth for scrubbing.
- Section Two (Insight On Demand, 3-point frosted capability panel, dual action buttons).
- Immediate entry CTA into the Brain Lab.

### 4.2 Six Content Regions (120 Total Ideas)
1. **01 — PERCEPTION**: Your brain isn't a camera (Change blindness, blind spots, saccadic masking, color constancy).
2. **02 — MEMORY**: Your life isn't stored like a video (DRM false memories, flashbulb distortions, reconsolidation).
3. **03 — THE SOCIAL BRAIN**: Other people get inside your head (Asch conformity, chameleon effect, emotional contagion).
4. **04 — HABITS, ATTENTION & MODERN LIFE**: Attention is negotiated (Infinite scroll, intermittent rewards, task switching).
5. **05 — BODY & SENSES**: Your body acts without asking (Proprioception, interoception, phantom limb, vestibular lag).
6. **06 — CONSCIOUSNESS, DECISIONS & WTF**: Deepest rabbit holes (Split-brain, Monty Hall probability, chronostasis).

### 4.3 Interactive Experiment Suite
- **Blind Spot Mapper**: Interactive canvas testing optic disc occlusion with distance guidance.
- **Change Blindness Flicker Spotter**: Alternating visual masks demonstrating inattentional blindness.
- **Stroop Effect Reaction Lab**: Cognitive interference test tracking millisecond reaction time latency.
- **False Memory Reconstructor**: Deese-Roediger-McDermott (DRM) word recall paradigm showing brain lure insertion.
- **Time Dilation Estimator**: Subjective time dilation under differing cognitive frequencies.
- **Monty Hall Probability Trap**: Three-door Bayesian probability paradox showing intuitive human error.

### 4.4 Centerpiece Experiences
- **The Brain vs The Internet**: Multi-stage interactive cascading loop simulation tracking digital attention hijack from notification to time loss with live neurotransmitter & focus telemetry.
- **Build Your Own Weird Brain**: Sandbox simulator with 7 Apple-style sliders (Novelty, Attention, Uncertainty, Social Pressure, Reward, Memory, Prediction) running behavioral simulations against real-world scenarios.

### 4.5 Rabbit Hole Knowledge Network
- Nonlinear exploration engine connecting every concept to 3–4 related rabbit holes.
- Traversal history and exploration stats tracking "rabbit holes dived".

## 5. Trust, Compliance & Legal Requirements
- **Cookie Consent**: Floating banner with granular toggles (Essential, Analytical, Experience) and local storage persistence.
- **Privacy Policy**: Comprehensive GDPR & CCPA disclosures, data minimization, cookie descriptions.
- **Terms & Conditions**: Academic educational disclaimer, liability boundaries, intellectual property terms.
- **Refund Policy**: Clear 30-day money-back guarantee terms for lab passes and digital research publications.
- **Cookies Policy**: Explicit technical explanation of local storage and session persistence.
- **Verified Business Details**: Physical address, registered legal entity, support email, and editorial council.
- **Zero Fake Claims**: Removal of fake reviews and unverified claims; all citations grounded in neuroscience.
- **Accessibility & Contrast**: Strict WCAG 2.1 AA/AAA compliance, screen-reader semantic landmarks, alt text on all media, full keyboard navigation.

---

## 6. Success Metrics
- **Engagement Depth**: Visitors exploring ≥ 3 rabbit holes per session.
- **Experiment Completion Rate**: ≥ 80% completion of initiated interactive experiments.
- **Performance**: < 1.5s First Contentful Paint, 60fps animations.
- **Accessibility**: 100% automated accessibility pass with zero contrast or missing alt violations.
